# One Body Temple — Logo Guidelines

> One page, written for anyone placing the mark. The wordmark is already the logo. This kit does not replace it.

## 1. The logo

- **Idea:** One continuous stroke. The O is a ring, its top runs straight into the bar, and that bar carries the B and the T. It reads as a single act, not three letters dropped next to each other.
- **Versions:**
  - Primary wordmark — `digital/obt-wordmark-black.svg` on light, `digital/obt-wordmark-white.svg` on dark
  - Stacked — wordmark over ONE BODY TEMPLE — `digital/obt-stacked-black.svg` / `obt-stacked-white.svg`
  - Symbol — the O alone, for favicons, avatars, and embroidery under 20 mm — `digital/obt-symbol-black.svg`
- **Files:** SVG in `digital/` for screens and print. Do not recreate the wordmark by typing “OBT”.

## 2. Clear space

Keep a clear zone of **1 × the bar** around the logo on every side. The bar is the horizontal stroke that runs from the O to the end of the T. The zone scales with the logo. Never use a fixed pixel gap.

Nothing else sits in that zone: no tagline closer than the bar’s height, no box, no photo detail.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | 120 px wide | 32 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 160 px wide | 40 mm wide |
| Symbol (the O) | 16 px | 6 mm |

Below those sizes, use the symbol, not the wordmark.

## 4. Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0E0E0E` | 14 14 14 | 0 0 0 95 | Black 6 C | Wordmark on light grounds, type |
| Bone | `#F3EFE7` | 243 239 231 | 0 2 5 5 | 9183 C | Paper, reversed-logo ground |
| Ember | `#C2410C` | 194 65 12 | 0 66 94 24 | 173 C | Accent only. Large type, rules, one-colour special use |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | — | Wordmark on Ink |

Pantone matches are approximations. Check a physical swatch before embroidery or spot-colour print.

**Approved pairs**

- Ink wordmark on Bone or white
- White wordmark on Ink
- Ember wordmark on Bone or Ink, for a single special use (a label, a stitch-out), never as the default
- White or Ink wordmark on a photograph, only on a calm area

Ember on Bone is about 4.5 : 1. Use it for large type and rules. Body copy stays Ink on Bone.

The live site ground is pure black (`#000000`). That is an approved dark ground, the same as Ink.

## 5. Typography

The wordmark is custom geometry. It is not a font.

- **Headlines:** Baskerville. Web: Libre Baskerville (SIL Open Font License).
- **Labels, UI, the “ONE BODY TEMPLE” descriptor:** Helvetica Neue Medium, tracked. Web: Inter Medium (SIL Open Font License).
- **Body:** Baskerville or Libre Baskerville at a normal size. Short sentences.

Do not set headlines in the same tracked sans as the descriptor. The descriptor is a label. The headline is a voice.

## 6. Don'ts

- Don’t stretch, squash, rotate, or italicise the mark.
- Don’t recolour it outside Ink, White, and Ember.
- Don’t add a shadow, outline, gradient, or glow.
- Don’t separate the O, B, and T, or change the bar.
- Don’t place it on a busy photo without a calm field.
- Don’t typeset “OBT” in a font and call it the logo.
- Don’t put the symbol and the wordmark side by side at the same size. The symbol replaces the wordmark only when space is too small.
