## Anchor line

> Your body is a temple. Honor it through how you live.

Other lines kept in rotation: *Built For His Glory* · *One Body. One Calling.* · *Built With Purpose.*

## Foundation

> Your body is a gift entrusted to you. What you do with it is your answer to the giver.

Most brands sell clothing. Some sell a lifestyle. OBT sells a calling.

## Vision

To ignite a generation that refuses to waste what they were given — a generation that trains with intention, carries themselves with purpose, and understands that how you care for your body is a declaration of what you believe your life is worth.

## Mission

One Body Temple creates premium apparel for people who believe the body is a gift: not to be neglected, not to be worshipped, but to be stewarded. Through discipline, excellence, and a life built on something greater than themselves, OBT inspires athletes and everyday individuals to train harder, live truer, and represent something worth representing.

## Five pillars

1. **Foundation** — The reason beneath the reason.
2. **Stewardship** — You did not make yourself. You are responsible for yourself.
3. **Discipline** — The willingness to be your own demanding standard.
4. **Excellence** — The refusal to put in less than you have.
5. **Purpose** — The compass that makes every decision easier.
